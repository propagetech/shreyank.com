Sound Recording

Production sound mixing, sync sound, and location recording.

Production sound mixing

On-set sync sound and multitrack recording for features, documentaries, and series.

Includes: boom and wireless lavs, multitrack mix to recorder, timecode sync, daily sound reports.

Sync sound

Capturing vocal performance on set so actors are not asked to re-voice in ADR later.

Location sound recording

Full location sound support—dialogue, plant mics, and noise coordination with production.

Ambience recording

MS, ORTF, and spaced-pair recordings for room tones, exteriors, and signature environments.

FX recording

Production effects recorded on location or in controlled environments.

Foley recording

Location and studio foley with attention to performance, perspective, and sync.
