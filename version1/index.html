Approach

Why directors and producers work with Shreyank

Film sound is listening under pressure—on a noisy street, in a moving vehicle, or when a take cannot be repeated. The work is technical discipline in service of performance: protecting dialogue, building usable ambience, and leaving post teams material they can trust.

Collaboration is direct and calm. Expect clear communication with production and editorial, sensible kit choices for each location, and editorial that serves the cut—not showreel moments.
